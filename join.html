Join the lab

We are a new lab and growing! We encourage intellectual diversity—biologists, systems/cognitive/theoretical neuroscientists, psychologists, engineers, artificial intelligence researchers, and others are all welcome. We are recruiting for the following positions:

Postdocs

Email Dr. Tian with a CV and cover letter, summarizing your prior work and future interests.

Graduate students

You have two options: (1) Apply to the NYU Neural Science Doctoral Program and mention Dr. Tian as someone you’d like to work with; (2) Apply directly to Dr. Tian through the NYU Tandon Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. program. In both cases, feel free to reach out to Dr. Tian beforehand.

Technicians/Research assistants

Email Dr. Tian with a CV and short description (½ page) of research experience and future interests.

Undergraduates

Email Dr. Tian with a CV and short description (½ page) of research experience and future interests.
