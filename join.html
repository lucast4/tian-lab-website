Work with us

We're always interested in hearing from curious, motivated people who want to study how the brain builds new ideas from old ones.

Graduate students

Prospective PhD students should apply through the Center for Neural Science or the Department of Biomedical Engineering admissions process, and are welcome to reach out beforehand to discuss research fit.

Postdoctoral researchers

Postdoc openings are posted here as they become available. If you're interested in behavioral, computational, or neurophysiological approaches to creative intelligence, get in touch with a CV and a short note about your research interests.

Undergraduate researchers

NYU undergraduates interested in gaining research experience are welcome to reach out directly. Please include your year, relevant coursework, and why you're interested in the lab's research.
