Lucas Tian

Principal Investigator

Lucas Tian leads the Tian Lab at NYU's Center for Neural Science and Department of Biomedical Engineering. His research examines how the brain rapidly generates structured novelty through abstraction and recombination — spanning behavioral, computational, and neurophysiological approaches.
